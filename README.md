# Customer Data Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing customer purchasing behavior,
revenue trends, and satisfaction scores across 4 product categories 
and 10 major Indian cities.

## Key Metrics
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Avg Satisfaction Score | 7.75 |
| Average Revenue | 142.00K |
| Top Product Category | Home Appliances |
| Regions Covered | 10 cities across India |

## Product Categories Analyzed
- Electronics
- Fashion
- Groceries
- Home Appliances

 Dashboard Features

 KPI Cards
- Average satisfaction score across all customers
- Average revenue per product category
- Top performing product category

 Visuals
- **Stacked Bar Chart** — Orders and Revenue by Product Category
- **Area Chart** — Revenue distribution by Region (10 cities)
- **Horizontal Bar Chart** — Revenue and Orders by Product Category
- **100% Stacked Bar Chart** — Max satisfaction score by Product Category
- **Map Visual** — Geographic revenue distribution across India
- **Slicers** — Filter by Customer Name and Product Category

 Key Insights
- Fashion leads in overall revenue (1.0M+)
- Home Appliances is the top product category
- Fashion achieves 100% max satisfaction score
- Revenue varies significantly across regions
